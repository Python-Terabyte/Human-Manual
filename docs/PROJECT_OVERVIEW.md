# Human Manual — Project Overview

> **"Because people don't come with instruction manuals."**

---

## What Is Human Manual?

Human Manual is a production-grade SaaS platform that lets individuals, friends, teams, companies, and communities create highly interactive personal manuals that explain who they are, how they think, how they communicate, and what drives them.

Think: **LinkedIn + Instagram + Employee Handbook + Personal Website** — alive, animated, and deeply personal.

---

## Core Value Proposition

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| New teammates feel like strangers for months | Read their Manual in minutes |
| Remote teams lack culture connection | Culture Wall, Spotlights, Birthday Wall |
| Onboarding is cold and generic | Personal Welcome Portals |
| Nobody knows how their colleagues prefer to communicate | "How To Work With Me" section |
| HR profiles are boring and unused | Beautiful, gamified, media-rich Manuals |

## User Types

### 1. Individual User
- Creates and owns a personal Manual
- Controls visibility (public / private / friends / custom)
- Can join organizations and friend circles
- Example: *Asim Saleem, Software Engineer, Lahore — shares his Manual publicly*

### 2. Company Admin
- Creates an organization workspace
- Invites employees, creates departments and teams
- Assigns onboarding templates
- Manages permissions and visibility policies
- Example: *HR Manager at TechCorp creates "Engineering" department with 40 employees*

### 3. Employee
- Builds a personal Manual within their company workspace
- Media uploads, personality widgets, career history
- Participates in culture challenges and spotlights
- Example: *John Doe, Junior Developer — completes his Manual in 20 minutes using AI assist*

### 4. Friend Circle Owner
- Creates private groups (family, friend squads, travel crew)
- Invites members via link or email
- Sets visibility and content rules
- Example: *"Weekend Crew" — 8 friends share personal fun-fact Manuals with each other*
